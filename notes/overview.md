# Overview

Pest Patrol is a browser shooting-gallery game set inside a Filipino sari-sari store. The player uses the mouse to aim and left-click to shoo cartoon mosquitoes before closing time. The goal is to hit as many mosquitoes as possible during one 60-second round and score the highest total.

A round starts as soon as the page loads. The HUD shows the score and countdown, mosquitoes spawn from the store interior, and each mosquito disappears when clicked. Small mosquitoes are worth 30 points, medium mosquitoes 20, and large mosquitoes 10. Mosquitoes also vanish on their own after a short time if the player misses them.

Controls:
- Move the mouse to aim.
- Left-click to shoo mosquitoes.
- Restart appears when the round ends.

Current status:
- Works: the store scene, mosquito spawning, hit detection, score tracking, countdown timer, game-over screen, restart reset, and on-screen instructions.
- Does not yet include: sound effects, power-ups, multiple levels, or saved high scores.
